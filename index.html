08 / Release boundary

Complete workflow. Contained first release.

The platform covers the full journey shown here, with clear limits on adjacent services and automation.

In the first release

✓Public calls, enquiries and intake
✓Reviewed AI drafts, agreements and e-signing
✓Client portal, scheduling and worker records
✓Route estimates and reviewed fee proposals
✓Office dashboards, activity history and export

Later, after separate approval

→Native app, live chat and autonomous AI sending
→Live navigation and route optimisation
→Automatic invoicing, claims, payroll and accounting
→DVA and aged care funding workflows
→High intensity and clinical services, once care and escalation processes are approved

Electronic signing uses an approved provider; the first release does not build its own signing engine.
